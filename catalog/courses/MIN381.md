## Course objectives

- 1. Explain how pastors’ calls, characters, and gifts prepare them for ministry.
- 2. Analyze the relationship of pastors’ devotional and personal lives to ministry.
- 3. Summarize principles to guide pastors in preaching and teaching.
- 4. Describe the pastoral role of worship in the church.
- 5. Examine the issues and principles related to pastoral care.
- 6. Explain the balance between the pastoral roles of leading and training.
- 7. Understand and appreciate the dynamics and effectiveness of cell group formation.
- 8. Acquire the basic steps and recognize the purpose of special services in the church.
- 9. Summarize principles for relating to lay leaders and staff.
- 10. Identify keys for maintaining rhythm and passion in ministry.
