## Course objectives

- 1. Discuss key events in the life and ministry of the apostle Paul, including the background issues that prompted his letter to the Roman church.
- 2. Defend Paul’s right to be called an apostle, missionary, theologian, and servant of Jesus Christ.
- 3. Describe how Paul develops in his letter to the Romans the gospel message involving people’s need of a Savior, God’s provision of a Savior, God’s shaping process, and the Holy Spirit’s empowering of the believer.
- 4. Evaluate Paul’s discourse on Israel’s election, unbelief, and restoration and relate this to his primary ministry to Gentiles.
- 5. Apply in your cultural context and in your own life Paul’s practical teaching with respect to living the Christian life and relating to all people, including non-Christians, authorities, and other believers.
- 6. Preach or teach the gospel message as expounded by the apostle Paul in his letter to the Roman church.

### Chapter 5: 5 - The Empowering Spirit: Romans 8

- **Life in the Spirit (Romans 8:1–17)**

  - `5.1.4` Based on given Scripture passages, contrast a “child” of God with a “child” of the devil, and describe characteristics of each one.
- **Future Glory (Romans 8:18–27)**
  - `5.2.1` Contrast the future glory God has prepared for His people with their present suffering, in the context of which creation, believers, and the Spirit groan.
- **More Than Conquerors (Romans 8:28–39)**
  - `5.3.1` Identify in Romans 8:28 five certainties about God that demonstrate His faithfulness to His people.
  - `5.3.2` Define given theological terms Paul uses in Romans 8:29–30 to explain God’s purpose for believers.
  - `5.3.3` Analyze five questions Paul poses in Romans 8:31–39 to determine their effectiveness in supporting his argument that God is faithful to His people.
