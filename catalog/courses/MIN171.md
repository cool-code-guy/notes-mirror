## Course objectives

- 1. Describe the need for a process to guide the church in vision and ministry.
- 2. Identify the five functions of the Acts 2 process and explain their roles in the Spirit-empowered church.
- 3. Explain the need for both strategic planning and the empowerment of the Spirit for church revitalization.
- 4. Explain the value of the priesthood of all believers and how utilizing every believer’s gifts can impact ministry in the local church.
- 5. Describe the necessity of an honest assessment of a local church’s strengths and weaknesses in order to grow that church in the five functions of the Acts 2 process.
- 6. Analyze the biblically based mission of the church and its role in vision and strategic planning.
- 7. Analyze the significance of both the Great Commandment and the Great Commission in fulfilling the mission of the church.
- 8. Describe the process for developing a God-inspired vision for the local church as it intersects with people’s needs in your community.
- 9. Identify the core values of your church, and write them down.
- 10. Develop a strategic plan for your church, utilizing the five functions in Acts 2 that are based on (1) the core values of the church and (2) a God-inspired vision for ministry.
