## Course objectives

- 1. Summarize the message and content of each of the five books.
- 2. Explain how Christians should relate to the laws of the Pentateuch.
- 3. Define the biblical concept of holiness and clean and unclean.
- 4. Point out the meaning and significance of the Sinai Covenant.
- 5. Outline the time line of major events covered in the Pentateuch.
- 6. Summarize the principles of God’s instructions for Israel in the areas of ceremony, worship, and atonement.

## Units

- **Book of Beginnings** -- chapters 1, 2, 3, 4
- **Becoming the People of God Through the Presence of God** -- chapters 5, 6, 7
- **Continuing Fellowship with the Holy God** -- chapters 8, 9
- **Learning to Obey God's Leading on the Journey** -- chapters 10, 11
- **Renewing God's Covenant Relationship with Israel** -- chapters 12, 13

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - Creation (Genesis 1–2)

- **Book of Genesis**
  - `1.1.1` Explain the major views on the authorship and dating of the book of Genesis.
  - `1.1.2` Outline the basic divisions of Genesis, and identify the differences in purpose of chapters 1 and 2.
  - `1.1.3` Summarize the purpose of Genesis.
- **Creation of the World (Genesis 1–2:3)**
  - `1.2.1` Identify four principles of the creation in Genesis 1.
  - `1.2.2` Define the concept of blessing in Genesis.
  - `1.2.3` Introduce the concept of the Sabbath in Genesis 2:2–3.
- **Creation of Humans (Genesis 1:26–28; 2)**
  - `1.3.1` Identify the focus of the Genesis creation account.
  - `1.3.2` Define the image of God in humans from the context of Genesis 1.
  - `1.3.3` List three principles of healthy relationships modeled by God.
  - `1.3.4` Point out the significance of God’s creating humans male and female.
  - `1.3.5` Analyze God’s use of the word us in Genesis 1.

### Chapter 2: 2 - The Fall and the Flood (Genesis 3–11)

- **The Fall (Genesis 3–5)**

  - `2.1.2` Describe the consequences of disobeying God.
  - `2.1.3` Describe how God gives hope to broken people facing His judgment.
- **The Flood (Genesis 6–9)**
  - `2.2.1` Describe how sin has increased since the Fall.
  - `2.2.2` Analyze what the Flood teaches about God.
  - `2.2.3` Tell what we learn about people from the Flood account.
  - `2.2.4` Explain the curse on Canaan.
- **After the Flood (Genesis 10–11)**
  - `2.3.1` Summarize the purpose of Genesis 10 and 11.
  - `2.3.2` Analyze the purpose of genealogies in the Bible.
  - `2.3.3` Identify spiritual lessons taught in Genesis 10 and 11.
