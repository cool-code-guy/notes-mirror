## Course objectives

- 1. Explain how God used the Jews, Greeks, and Romans to prepare the world for His Son.
- 2. Analyze the political, social, and religious characteristics of New Testament times.
- 3. Explain the formation of the New Testament canon.
- 4. Identify five nations, five districts, ten cities, and five bodies of water related to New Testament times.
- 5. Divide the New Testament into four groups, listing the books and characteristics of each group.
- 6. Summarize how Jesus Christ is a unifying theme of the New Testament.
- 7. Analyze the authorship and historical context of each New Testament book.
- 8. Explain and apply the theme and purpose of each New Testament book.

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - An Overview of the New Testament

- **Preparation for the New Testament**
  - `1.1.1` Explain how God used the Jews, Greeks, and Romans to prepare the world for His Son.
  - `1.1.2` Define Pharisees, Sadducees, Sanhedrin, and Septuagint.
- **Setting of the New Testament: Palestine in the First Century**
  - `1.2.1` Describe six rulers of Herod’s family, the high priest, the temple, and synagogues.
  - `1.2.2` Discuss the origins of the New Testament.
- **Books of the New Testament**
  - `1.3.1` Identify the four groups of New Testament books, placing each book in its proper group.
- **Christ in Every Book of the New Testament**
  - `1.4.1` Summarize three pictures of Christ from each group of New Testament books.

### Chapter 2: 2 - The Synoptic Gospels: Matthew, Mark, and Luke

- **The Gospel of Matthew**
  - `2.1.1` Explain Matthew’s purpose and the main way he fulfilled it.
  - `2.1.2` Identify and explain the five teaching segments or sermons of Matthew.
  - `2.1.3` Explain why Matthew emphasized the Kingdom. State three ways he stressed it.
- **The Gospel of Mark**
  - `2.2.1` Explain Mark’s purpose and how he fulfilled it.
  - `2.2.2` Give two examples of how Mark helped Gentile readers.
- **The Gospel of Luke**
  - `2.3.1` Contrast the purposes of Matthew and Luke.
  - `2.3.2` Identify five things found only in Luke that emphasize his theme of Jesus, the Savior of all.
- **Characteristics of the Synoptics**
  - `2.4.1` Summarize and apply four reasons Jesus taught in parables.
  - `2.4.2` Define the kingdom of God, and explain its two phases.
  - `2.4.3` Explain why Jesus healed and did miracles.

### Chapter 3: 3 - The Gospel of John

- **Understanding John**
  - `3.1.1` Explain why John omitted many things that are in the Synoptics.
  - `3.1.2` Analyze the words “signs,” “believe,” and “life” in relation to John’s purpose.
- **The Structure of John**
  - `3.2.1` Analyze the introduction and conclusion of John.
  - `3.2.2` 
- **The Teachings of Jesus in John**
  - `3.3.1` Analyze the relationship between the signs and teachings in John 5, 6, 9, and 11.
  - `3.3.2` Identify the seven “I am” statements in John.
  - `3.3.3` Explain three lessons from people who talked with Jesus.

### Chapter 4: 4 - The Book of Acts

- **Understanding the Book of Acts**
  - `4.1.1` Identify the title, author, and date of Acts.
  - `4.1.2` Summarize five purposes that Acts serves.
- **Outlining the Book of Acts**
  - `4.2.1` Divide Acts into three parts, giving the chapters for each part.
- **Witnessing about Jesus**
  - `4.3.1` Indicate three steps in which believers witnessed for Jesus from Jerusalem to Rome.

### Chapter 5: 5 - Letters about the Gospel and Faith: Romans and Galatians

- **Paul**
  - `5.1.1` Summarize Paul’s background, conversion, and public ministry.
  - `5.1.2` Identify the four groups of Paul’s letters and the books in each.
- **Romans**
  - `5.2.1` Describe the setting for the book of Romans.
  - `5.2.2` Relate righteousness to the five parts of Romans.
- **Galatians**
  - `5.3.1` Explain why Paul wrote to the Galatian churches.
  - `5.3.2` Summarize three reasons Paul gave the Galatians for believing the gospel.

### Chapter 6: 6 - Letters about Church Problems: 1 and 2 Corinthians

- **First Corinthians**
  - `6.1.1` Describe the geographical, social, and religious setting of Corinth.
  - `6.1.2` Identify four problems and four questions of the Corinthians.
- **Second Corinthians**
  - `6.2.1` Trace the flow of 2 Corinthians from Ephesus to Macedonia to Corinth.
  - `6.2.2` Contrast Paul with the false apostles in at least three ways.

### Chapter 7: 7 - Letters from Prison: Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, and Philemon

- **Ephesians**
  - `7.1.1` Describe the background of Ephesus. Include its location, business, temple, and church.
  - `7.1.2` Analyze four relationships Paul wrote about in Ephesians.
- **Philippians**
  - `7.2.1` Summarize the background of Paul’s letter to the Philippians.
  - `7.2.2` Summarize what Philippians teaches about joy and unity.
- **Colossians**
  - `7.3.1` Analyze the Colossian heresy and Paul’s teachings on it.
  - `7.3.2` Explain Paul’s teaching about old and new clothes.
- **Philemon**
  - `7.4.1` Analyze three people and two lessons in the letter to Philemon.

### Chapter 8: 8 - Letters about the Future: 1 and 2 Thessalonians

- **First Thessalonians**
  - `8.1.1` Summarize the background of Paul’s first letter to the Thessalonians.
  - `8.1.2` Identify the theme, purposes, and three main parts of 1 Thessalonians.
- **Second Thessalonians**
  - `8.2.1` Summarize and apply what 2 Thessalonians teaches about the Rapture, Antichrist, and Second Coming.

### Chapter 9: 9 - Letters for Pastors: 1 and 2 Timothy and Titus

- **First Timothy**
  - `9.1.1` Summarize Timothy’s parentage, conversion, and relationship to Paul.
  - `9.1.2` Identify at least three topics Paul wrote about in 1 Timothy.
- **Titus**

  - `9.2.2` Summarize what Titus teaches about grace and the reputation of the gospel.
- **Second Timothy**
  - `9.3.1` Explain Paul’s comparison of a pastor to a soldier, an athlete, and a farmer.
  - `9.3.2` Summarize what Paul taught about the last days and the Scriptures

  - `10.1.3` Summarize and apply the purpose of the seven warnings in Hebrews.

- **First John**

- **Second John**

  - `11.2.2` Explain the relationship between love and truth in 2 John.
- **Third John**
  - `11.3.1` Describe the spiritual character of Gaius, Diotrephes, and Demetrius.

### Chapter 12: 12 - Letters about False Teaching, Part 2: 2 Peter and Jude
