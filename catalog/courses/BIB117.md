## Course objectives

- 1. Summarize background information related to the Prison Epistles.
- 2. Identify key facts related to authorship, dating, and the recipients of these letters.
- 3. Identify the major themes of each letter, and explain how these themes can apply to modern believers.
- 4. Describe the organizational structure of each epistle, identifying major divisions.
- 5. Recount key issues addressed in the Prison Epistles, such as conflicts, false teaching, and church successes.
- 6. Identify key Christian doctrines addressed by Paul, and describe how Paul develops these teachings.
