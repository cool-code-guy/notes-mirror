## Course objectives

- 1. Comment on the author, date, setting, structure, and purposes of Acts. Analyze the extent to which Luke’s purposes have been fulfilled in your life and church.
- 2. Relate the sequence, content, and theology of Acts to the rest of the New Testament. Compare the theology of Acts with your theology.
- 3. Analyze five places in Acts where believers were first filled with the Holy Spirit.
- 4. Identify the church’s problems and solutions in growing from one language and culture to many.
- 5. Identify the main people, cities, and regions in Acts.
- 6. Explain how Acts gives the background and setting of several New Testament letters.
- 7. Trace the steps by which the gospel spread from Jerusalem to Rome.
- 8. Identify and apply the main reasons for church growth in Acts.
- 9. Apply the principles of guidance in Acts to making decisions today.
- 10. Illustrate from Acts that God uses hard times for His purposes. Apply this principle to believers today.
- 11. Identify and apply some of the missionary principles in Acts.
- 12. Trace Paul’s travels from Antioch to Rome.

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - Understanding Acts

- **Approaching Acts**
  - `1.1.1` Analyze the title, author, and date of Acts.
- **Appreciating Acts**
  - `1.2.1` Explain how Acts bridges the Gospels and Pauline Letters.
  - `1.2.2` Identify how Acts traces the growth of the church.
  - `1.2.3` Discuss how Acts serves as a guide for faith and apologetics.
  - `1.2.4` Indicate how the Holy Spirit is key to witnessing.
- **Analyzing Acts**
  - `1.3.1` Explain two ways to outline Acts.

### Chapter 2: 2 - From the Resurrection to Pentecost

- **The Gift Our Father Promised**
  - `2.1.1` Analyze the promise, the command, the gift, and the Baptizer in Acts 1.
- **The Work of the Church**
  - `2.2.1` Explain the plan, the place, the power, and the purpose of witnessing. Relate these to self and others.
- **Steps to the Baptism in the Holy Spirit**
  - `2.3.1` Identify four attitudes or actions that precede the outpouring of the Holy Spirit.

### Chapter 3: 3 - The Holy Spirit Is Poured Out

- **Pentecost Fulfilled**
  - `3.1.1` Explain how the Day of Pentecost fulfills the Old Testament Feast of Pentecost and launches the Pentecostal era.
- **Signs of Spirit Baptism**
  - `3.2.1` Distinguish between two unique signs of the Spirit’s presence at Pentecost and the initial physical evidence of Spirit baptism that continues today.
- **Four Purposes of Speaking in Tongues**
  - `3.3.1` Analyze four purposes for speaking in tongues and demonstrate at least two in your own life.

### Chapter 4: 4 - The Birth of the Church

- **Responses to Pentecost**
  - `4.1.1` Describe five typical responses to Pentecost.
- **The Pattern of Peter’s Preaching**
  - `4.2.1` Identify four practical methods and three vital messages from Peter’s sermon on the Day of Pentecost and their significance for those who witness or preach.
- **The Biblical Pattern of a Dynamic Church**
  - `4.3.1` Identify thirteen characteristics that mark a powerful and effective church.

### Chapter 5: 5 - Peter and John Heal a Lame Man

- **Embracing the Needs Around Us**
  - `5.1.1` Summarize and apply the lessons Acts 3:1–10 teaches about our responsibility toward others.
- **Explaining the Gospel**
  - `5.2.1` Analyze the two major themes of Peter’s message to the Sanhedrin.
- **Enduring Hardship and Testing**
  - `5.3.1` Generate an appropriate response for why Christians sometimes suffer for doing good.

### Chapter 6: 6 - The Growth of the Church

- **The Purifying of the Church**
  - `6.1.1` Summarize specific ways Christians showed concern about meeting needs within the Body and what you can learn from their example.
  - `6.1.2` Explain why Ananias and Sapphira were judged so severely.
- **The Powerful Church**
  - `6.2.1` Describe five distinguishing marks of a powerful church.
- **The Persistent Church**
  - `6.3.1` Understand three tactics Satan uses to attack the church, and be on guard against such assaults in your own life.

### Chapter 7: 7 - Stephen: What Good Can Come of Trouble?

- **Conflict in the Church**
  - `7.1.1` Identify the internal problems the church was facing in Acts 6.
  - `7.1.2` Describe the methods used by the apostles in handling the first church problem and apply the same methods to problems that may arise in your church.
- **Stephen’s Arrest and Trial**
  - `7.2.1` Justify why Stephen’s pointed sermon was necessary.
- **The First Martyr**
  - `7.3.1` Explain the significance of Stephen’s death and its application to your own life.

### Chapter 8: 8 - Scattered Seed: The Church Expands

- **Mass Evangelism**
  - `8.1.1` Describe what caused the church to begin evangelism efforts outside Jerusalem.
- **Personal Evangelism**
  - `8.2.1` Discuss the significance of Philip’s ministry to the lone Ethiopian eunuch, listing at least two important evangelism principles.
- **Seed for Future Harvests**
  - `8.3.1` Explain the effects of Saul’s persecution, conversion, and early ministry in the church.

### Chapter 9: 9 - The Acts of the Apostles

- **Peter’s Expanding Ministry**
  - `9.1.1` Summarize the stages of the church’s expansion to full-fledged ministry to the Gentiles and the implications of the change for the church.
- **Antioch: The Changing Shape of the Church**
  - `9.2.1` Describe the new methods, leaders, and mission of the Antioch church and the example this church sets for churches today.
- **Jerusalem: The Difficult Will of God**
  - `9.3.1` Identify five reasons why Christians suffer.

### Chapter 10: 10 - The First Missionary Journey

- **To Galatia: On the Cutting Edge of Spiritual Growth**
  - `10.1.1` Understand the spiritual climate that led to Barnabas and Paul’s first missionary journey, and list seven principles of missions.
- **The Galatian Ministry: How to Minister Successfully**
  - `10.2.1` Apply six keys for overcoming difficulties in ministry.
- **The Jerusalem Council: A Biblical Pattern of Conflict Resolution**
  - `10.3.1` Outline a biblical pattern for conflict resolution in the church.

### Chapter 11: 11 - The Second Missionary Journey

- **The Second Missionary Journey Begins**
  - `11.1.1` Identify five lessons for those who want to follow God’s leading based on Paul’s experiences in Acts 15:36–16:40.
- **A Tale of Three Cities**
  - `11.2.1` Contrast Paul’s approaches in preaching the gospel in the different cities as well as the manner in which the message was received in each city.
- **Corinth: The Final Leg of the Journey**
  - `11.3.1` Understand the unique challenges to ministry in Corinth and three ways God helped Paul meet those challenges.

### Chapter 12: 12 - Paul’s Third Missionary Journey

- **Ephesus: Battle for a City**
  - `12.1.1` List and explain ten key elements for winning the spiritual battle for a city.
- **From Ephesus to Jerusalem: The Rest of the Journey**
  - `12.2.1` Summarize Paul’s review of his own ministry: accomplishments, personal example for ministry, and warnings for the future.

### Chapter 13: 13 - Paul’s Journey to Rome

- **Paul’s Imprisonment in Jerusalem**
  - `13.1.1` Explain the issues surrounding the Jews’ assault on Paul in the temple and his incarceration by the Roman military.
  - `13.1.2` Identify principles from Paul’s experiences related to God’s calling Christians into ministry.
- **Paul’s Imprisonment in Caesarea**
  - `13.2.1` Recount key events and issues related to Paul’s imprisonment and trials in Caesarea.
- **Paul’s Imprisonment in Rome**
  - `13.3.1` Explain the purpose that Luke may have had in recording Paul’s speeches in these chapters and for documenting so many details of Paul’s journey to Rome.
  - `13.3.2` Describe Paul’s journey by sea from Caesarea to Rome and recount significant events from this journey.
  - `13.3.3` Describe what these examples of hardship might teach us about evangelism in our world today.
