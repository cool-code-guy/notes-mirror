## Course objectives

- 1. Explain the biblical foundations for ministerial ethics.
- 2. Describe the areas of a minister’s personal life that must be strengthened to ensure long-term ministry and avoid moral transgression.
- 3. Identify common pitfalls of moral transgression and practical steps a minister can take to avoid them.
- 4. Describe the principles involved in working with other ministers and churches in a healthy, courteous, and life-giving manner.
- 5. Define the concept of Voluntary Cooperative Fellowship and the relationship a minister has with the Fellowship.
- 6. Identify the healthy characteristics of both being married and being single in ministry.
- 7. Describe the key values of raising children who love the Lord and His church.
- 8. Describe ways a church or ministry can incorporate systems in the church to protect people and resources.
- 9. Explain how to execute key ministry responsibilities in an ethical manner that builds the body of Christ.
- 10. Identify how a twenty-first century minister can maintain proper boundaries in digital communications and social media.
- 11. Describe a process for making ethical decisions.

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1

- **A Basic Understanding of Ethical Systems**
  - `1.1.1` Define teleological and deontological ethics and explain how Christian ethics draws from both while being grounded in Scripture.
- **The Biblical Foundation for Ministerial Ethics**
  - `1.2.1` Identify the biblical books that provide specific wisdom regarding ministerial ethics, particularly the Pastoral Epistles.
- **Guarding the Gospel Message**
  - `1.3.1` Explain Paul's instructions to Timothy about guarding the gospel and describe what it means for ministry to be incarnational.
- **Avoiding Stumbling Blocks in Ministry**
  - `1.4.1` Describe Paul's model of integrity in ministry and identify the rights he set aside for the sake of the gospel.
- **Shepherding the Flock of God**
  - `1.5.1` Summarize Peter's instruction to church leaders and describe the posture of eagerness and servanthood in ministry.
- **Qualifications for Ministry Leadership**
  - `1.6.1` Explain the qualifications for ministry found in the Pastoral Epistles, including the importance of temperament, emotional health, and spiritual maturity.

### Chapter 2: 2

- **Different Types of Ethical Issues**
  - `2.1.1` Explain why developing transferable ethical decision-making skills is essential for remaining ethical in a changing world.
  - `2.1.2` Identify the role of spiritual leadership (e.g., district or network leaders) in guiding ethical decision-making for ministers.
- **A Process for Making Ethical Decisions**
  - `2.2.1` Identify what must guide the ethical decision-making process (prayer and dependence on God).
  - `2.2.2` Describe the steps of the ethical decision-making process (Clarify, Understand, Know the Rules, Look at Options, Act) and explain each step.

### Chapter 3: 3

- **Keeping Your Spiritual Tank Filled**
  - `3.1.1` Identify the importance of community, Sabbath, and spiritual disciplines in maintaining a minister's overall health.
- **Keeping Your Physical Tank Filled**
  - `3.2.1` Explain what Paul said to Timothy about physical training and articulate the appropriate balance between spiritual and physical health.
- **Keeping Your Emotional and Mental Health Tank Filled**
  - `3.3.1` Describe how God uses the Sabbath to teach trust and rest, and identify its role in building a sustainable ministry lifestyle.
- **Keeping Your Intellectual Tank Filled**
  - `3.4.1` Explain why building a lifestyle of learning is important for ministers and describe how it helps maintain sharpness and readiness.
- **The Enemy of Our Souls**
  - `3.5.1` Describe the core of building an ethical, intentional lifestyle (staying in tune with the Holy Spirit) and identify the role of spiritual disciplines in achieving spiritual health.

### Chapter 4: 4

- **Origins of Power**
  - `4.1.1` Explain what it means to exercise servant leadership modeled after Jesus, and describe how Jesus used His power to serve.
- **Good Leadership**
  - `4.2.1` Identify the ethical implications of empowering others in ministry leadership.
- **Spiritually Abusive Practices**
  - `4.3.1` Describe the role of team dynamics and collaboration in ethical ministry leadership.
- **Assumption of Dictatorial Authority**
  - `4.4.1` Define dictatorial authority and explain why it violates ethical ministry principles.
- **The Effect of Narcissism**
  - `4.5.1` Identify how insecurity and fauxnerability undermine authentic leadership.
- **Avoiding Spiritually Abusive Practices**
  - `4.6.1` Explain how Jesus modeled power through service and describe the implications for a minister's use of authority and influence.
- **Leaving an Abusive Environment**
  - `4.7.1` Describe the importance of genuine vulnerability in ministry and explain how it fosters authentic relationships.

### Chapter 5: 5

- **The Problem of Clergy Sexual Abuse**
  - `5.1.1` Explain why the church must take clergy sexual misconduct seriously, including the devastating effects it has on congregations.
  - `5.1.2` Identify resources and practical steps for ministers seeking accountability and help with pornography.
  - `5.1.3` Describe how abuse of authority and lack of boundaries contribute to ministerial failure, and identify the hidden dangers of pornography.
- **What Constitutes Clergy Sexual Misconduct**
  - `5.2.1` Describe how clergy sexual misconduct affects not only victims but also other congregants and the broader church.
  - `5.2.2` Identify the hidden dangers of pornography and explain how it erodes the soul and promotes harmful views of sexuality.
  - `5.2.3` Explain how clergy sexual misconduct spreads harm beyond direct victims and describe the minister's responsibility to prevent it.
- **Establishing Healthy Boundaries**
  - `5.3.1` Explain the value of boundaries and describe how they provide clarity and protect emotional and relational health.
  - `5.3.2` Identify appropriate boundaries for single ministers and describe how they help avoid compromising situations.
  - `5.3.3` Identify essential boundaries for children and youth ministries, including the 'Never Alone' rule.
- **Resisting Sexual Temptation**
  - `5.4.1` Identify practical skills for recognizing and resisting sexual temptation, including awareness of personal vulnerabilities.
- **Biblical Sexuality**
  - `5.5.1` Explain the biblical foundation for human sexuality (Genesis 2:24) and describe how it informs a minister's ethical commitments.

### Chapter 6: 6

- **Tithing and Taxes**
  - `6.1.1` Explain how tithing reflects spiritual dependence on God and why ministers are expected to model financial integrity through tithing and paying taxes.
- **Living Within Your Means**
  - `6.2.1` Identify practical safeguards for using credit cards and describe how establishing a budget supports financial integrity.
- **Gambling**
  - `6.3.1` Describe how the principle of 'two eyes on every transaction' protects churches from financial misconduct.
- **Preparation for the Future**
  - `6.4.1` Explain two key ways to prepare for the future (saving and investing) and describe how financial planning supports long-term ministerial sustainability.
- **A Minister’s Ethical Duty Regarding Ministry Finances**
  - `6.5.1` Describe how ministers should navigate issues of compensation, particularly in relation to integrity, fairness, and avoiding financial entitlement.

### Chapter 7: 7

- **Maintaining a Vibrant Marriage While in Ministry**
  - `7.1.1` Explain the minister's ethical priorities: family first, then congregation, and identify what it means to lead with integrity in the home.
  - `7.1.2` Describe the pressures faced by ministers' children and explain the ethical responsibility ministers have to protect their families.
  - `7.1.3` Identify unique challenges ministers face in work/family balance and explain what it means to lead a healthy family life.
- **Parenting in Ministry: God’s Blueprint for the Home**
  - `7.2.1` Explain what it means for ministry to come before family at times, and describe how ministers navigate the tension between calling and family.
  - `7.2.2` Explain Paul's exhortation in Ephesians 5 and describe what mutual submission looks like in a minister's marriage.
- **Single Adults and Ministry Ethics**
  - `7.3.1` Describe the Assemblies of God's position on employment issues such as fair pay, marital status, and employee rights, and explain how these reflect ethical commitments to justice.

### Chapter 8: 8

- **Being a Good Teammate**
  - `8.1.1` Identify the types of discipline that dismissed or suspended ministers face and explain how these restrictions protect the integrity of the AG fellowship.
- **Normalizing Men and Women Serving Together**
  - `8.2.1` Describe the role of the AG district and national leadership in overseeing ministerial integrity and explain the importance of accountability structures.
- **Treating Your Ministry Leader with Respect**
  - `8.3.1` Explain how conflicts within the church should be handled using AG resources and the ethical decision-making process.
- **Exercising Matthew 18 Principles and Ministerial Courtesy**
  - `8.5.1` Describe the AG's complementarian position on gender and explain how it is grounded in a biblical view of human sexuality.

  - `9.1.2` Identify key benefits of holding ministerial credentials with the Assemblies of God, including affirmation of calling, access to ministry opportunities, and relational support.
  - `9.1.3` Explain the responsibilities that come with choosing to belong to the Assemblies of God Fellowship.
  - `9.1.4` Describe the ethical importance of maintaining doctrinal agreement with the Sixteen Fundamental Truths and honoring other Fellowship commitments, such as abstinence from harmful substances and eschatological boundaries.

  - `9.2.1` Explain the importance of active participation and loyalty in strengthening the Fellowship and responding ethically in times of disagreement.
- **Accountability and Ministerial Discipline**
  - `9.3.1` Explain the purpose of the Assemblies of God's ministerial discipline process and the responsibilities of ministers within that structure.

- **Communicating the Message**

  - `10.2.1` Describe appropriate ways for ministers to engage with social media and digital tools while maintaining integrity and avoiding compromise.

  - `10.4.1` Explain the ethical responsibility of ministers to protect their ultimate allegiance to the Kingdom of God over political affiliation.

  - `10.6.1` Identify the benefits of AI for ministers (e.g., automating administrative tasks) while recognizing its limitations in replacing spiritual discernment.
