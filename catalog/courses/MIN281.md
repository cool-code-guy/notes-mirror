## Course objectives

- 1. Explain the significant place managing conflict has in the work of ministry.
- 2. Identify varying theories concerning the best approaches to managing and resolving conflict.
- 3. List and explain the various levels of conflict and how they differ from one another.
- 4. Explain how a minister can position himself or herself in a conflict for greatest congregational health.
- 5. Identify key biblical passages that give guidance to attitude and behavior in times of conflict and can aid the resolution of conflict.
- 6. Explain key factors that exist in communities and churches that give additional cause for conflict.
- 7. Identify key principles that aid leaders as they help others manage conflict.
- 8. Explain how a church’s size, community, and culture become catalysts for conflict.
- 9. List effective ground rules for conflict management.
- 10. Explain the difference between destructive and constructive conflict and how to respond when conflict reaches a destructive level.

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - The Conflict Issue

- **Ministry Has Changed**
  - `1.1.1` Define cultural changes and their impact on the ministry of the local church.
  - `1.1.2` Explain the four main trends in American society and how they contribute to modern challenges of pastoral ministry.
- **Pastors Who Leave Local Church Ministry**
  - `1.2.1` Identify the five dominant reasons for pastors leaving local church ministry.
  - `1.2.2` Describe Dresselhaus’s model for conflict resolution.
- **Conflict—The Number One Reason Pastors Quit**
  - `1.3.1` Explain the essential nature and definition of conflict.
  - `1.3.2` Identify the three phases of conflict and how to approach conflict at each phase.
- **The Basics of Conflict Management**
  - `1.4.1` Identify Paul’s three steps for conflict management given to Titus (3:10–11).
  - `1.4.2` Describe a pastor’s six principles for addressing conflict situations.
- **Conflict, the Bible, and You**
  - `1.5.1` Identify and explain scriptural guidelines for behavior in the midst of conflict.
  - `1.5.2` Identify the most effective tool for church conflict and explain how it is to be used.

### Chapter 2: 2 - The Evaluation of Conflict

- **Early Prognosis of Conflict**
  - `2.1.1` Identify the eight warning signs of disruptive church conflict and be able to characterize each accurately.
  - `2.1.2` Explain how the emergence of new leaders can bring or reveal disruptive conflict.
- **Evaluating the Depth of Conflict**
  - `2.2.1` Describe the essential elements of a constructive win / win approach to addressing conflict and a destructive win / lose approach.
  - `2.2.2` Name and define five levels of conflict that need to be addressed in differing ways.
- **Your Style in Handling Conflict**
  - `2.3.1` Explain the three common types of conflict people will experience in a church and how they can be given aid in resolving such conflicts.
  - `2.3.2` Identify and define the five primary ways of dealing with conflict.

### Chapter 3: 3 - The Study of Conflict

- **Different Levels of Conflict Require Different Types of Responses**
  - `3.1.1` Distinguish between Leas’s five levels of conflict, and demonstrate understanding of how to address each level.
  - `3.1.2` Explain the three principles that must govern conflict resolution at all levels and how to proceed when these principles are neglected.
- **Understanding Basic Conflict Issues**
  - `3.2.1` Identify the foundation for conflict contained in the essence of ministry.
  - `3.2.2` Recognize and be prepared for local church situations that can bring conflict.
- **Understanding You and What You Do**
  - `3.3.1` Identify the emotional challenges that are a regular part of ministry life and how they join with cultural influences in adding to the potential for conflict.
  - `3.3.2` Describe the ways in which both the pastor and the congregation can elevate the potential for conflict situations.

### Chapter 4: 4 - The Different Types of Conflict

- **Keeping Conflict Under Control**
  - `4.1.1` Define and demonstrate the biblical concept of confrontation.
  - `4.1.2` Identify the helpful components that gentleness brings to a conflict resolution.
- **Congregations that Are Having Conflict**
  - `4.2.1` Identify the underlying elements common to congregational conflict.
  - `4.2.2` List suggested ways of dealing with congregational conflict.
- **Unique Challenges in Different Size Churches**
  - `4.3.1` Identify and explain the differing dynamics found in churches of various sizes.
  - `4.3.2` Define the changes the role of pastor makes as a church grows.
- **Confrontation**
  - `4.4.1` Describe and apply the characteristics of conflict and confrontation experienced by Nehemiah.
  - `4.4.2` Identify the biblical recommendations for confrontation.

### Chapter 5: 5 - The Health of Conflict

- **Keeping the Goal in Mind**
  - `5.1.1` Identify and define the goals to achieve on the path of reconciliation.
  - `5.1.2` List and demonstrate the ten steps to be taken in preparing for reconciliation.
- **Conflict Can Be Helpful**
  - `5.2.1` Understand and explain a healthy approach to dealing with conflict.
  - `5.2.2` Define occasions where conflict cannot be fully resolved, and explain how to approach such circumstances.
- **Conflict Can Grow a Healthy Church**
  - `5.3.1` Identify and explain how a church’s conflict management style indicates its overall spiritual and emotional health.
  - `5.3.2` List and explain the steps a leader can take in increasing the health of his or her group in managing conflict.
- **Preventing Staff Infection**
  - `5.4.1` Understand and explain how ministry transition can add to the potential for conflict among the pastoral staff.
  - `5.4.2` Identify and define the steps a senior pastor can take to limit the potential for unhealthy conflict among the pastoral staff.

### Chapter 6: 6 - The Addressing of Conflict

- **Getting a Grip on Resolving Conflict**
  - `6.1.1` Explain the impact of prolonged conflict and some of the reasons it can exist.
  - `6.1.2` Identify and explain the steps a minister can take to be at his or her best when facing conflict.
- **Nipping Conflict in the Bud**
  - `6.2.1` Describe and justify the need to handle conflict quickly.
  - `6.2.2` Explain the example of conflict resolution in the early church, and identify key points for modern application.
- **Understanding Escalation in Conflict**
  - `6.3.1` Identify and explain the key reasons why normal conflict can move to aggressive and destructive conflict.
  - `6.3.2` List the steps of strategy a leader can take to address aggressive and destructive conflict.

### Chapter 7: 7 - The Destruction that Conflict Can Bring

- **Knowing the Difference between Constructive and Destructive Conflict**
  - `7.1.1` List and explain the key differences between destructive and constructive conflict.
  - `7.1.2` List and explain the key differences between criticism and gossip.
- **The Danger of Division**
  - `7.2.1` Describe and illustrate how division is ultimately destructive to a church.
  - `7.2.2` Explain the biblical antidote for division within the church.
- **Pastors Understand How the Enemy Attacks**
  - `7.3.1` Identify and explain key ways that the enemy battles against the church and its leaders.
  - `7.3.2` Summarize a strategy for dealing with these attacks in a manner that preserves the unity of the body of Christ.
