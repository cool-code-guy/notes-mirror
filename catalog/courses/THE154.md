## Course objectives

- 1. Explain the societal and spiritual circumstances that led to the Pentecostal revival of the early 1900s.
- 2. Recount key facts pertaining to the Pentecostal Movement and leading up to the birth of the Assemblies of God.
- 3. Identify central leaders and influencers during defining moments in the Assemblies of God’s history.
- 4. Summarize doctrines, beliefs, and values that define the Assemblies of God and distinguish it from other evangelical and Pentecostal organizations.
- 5. Trace the development of Assemblies of God missions from the beginning to the present and identify factors that have contributed to its remarkable growth and success.
- 6. Outline the histories, missions, and impact of various Assemblies of God programs, ministries, and educational institutions.
- 7. Evaluate the associations that impacted and were impacted by the Assemblies of God over the years.
- 8. Describe key areas of transformation in the Assemblies of God today and explain what must be done to meet the challenges of the future.
- 9. Describe the events that led to the formation of the Statement of Fundamental Truths.
- 10. Explain the structure and organization of the Assemblies of God.
- 11. Identify the Assemblies of God’s historical emphasis on the ministry of the Holy Spirit: baptism and gifts.
- 12. Analyze and apply lessons and biblical principles learned from this course to life and ministry.
