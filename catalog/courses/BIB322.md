## Course objectives

- 1. Apply principles of sound biblical interpretation.
- 2. Identify literary devices found in the Poetic Books and explain their significance.
- 3. Summarize the contributions of wisdom writings to living a full Christian life in contemporary cultures and contexts.
- 4. Identify the form, content, and social context of wisdom writings.
- 5. Compare and contrast Israel’s wisdom tradition with that of her neighbors.
- 6. Describe the compilation of Job, Psalms, and Proverbs.
- 7. Explain the fundamental tension in wisdom writings between doctrinal belief and life experiences.
- 8. Identify major themes of wisdom tradition and ways they, at times, stand in tension with one another.
- 9. Summarize the special contributions of individual wisdom writers.
- 10. Identify ways wisdom tradition influenced and shaped New Testament faith.

## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - Introduction to Hebrew Poetry

- **Reading the Bible**
  - `1.1.1` Explain why the Bible is our guide for living.
  - `1.1.2` Explain why understanding the progression of revelation is important when studying the Poetic Books.
  - `1.1.3` Define the principle of interpretation that states, “Scripture must interpret Scripture.”
- **Understanding Poetic Genre**
  - `1.2.1` Define literary genre, and explain its importance in biblical interpretation.
  - `1.2.2` Identify characteristics of Hebrew poetry.
  - `1.2.3` Describe the poetry/prose continuum.
- **Recognizing Poetic Devices**
  - `1.3.1` Describe poetic devices of Hebrew poetry.
  - `1.3.2` Compare the use of repetition in English and Hebrew literature.
  - `1.3.3` Identify four types of parallelism common to Hebrew poetry.
  - `1.3.4` Explain the challenges of translating chiasm.

### Chapter 2: 2 - Wisdom in Poetry

- **Introduction to Wisdom**
  - `2.1.1` Explain why wisdom literature is valuable for believers.
  - `2.1.2` Explain why wisdom literature may be called “spirituality for the marketplace.”
- **Wisdom’s Form and Function**
  - `2.2.1` Identify the two forms of wisdom literature, and describe wisdom’s content.
  - `2.2.2` Describe how wisdom writers responded to tension between experience and belief.
- **The Wisdom Tradition**
  - `2.3.1` Identify ways wisdom writers emphasize the importance of this world.

  - `2.3.4` Explain how Israel’s wisdom tradition is unique.
  - `2.3.5` Summarize the development of Israel’s wisdom tradition.
