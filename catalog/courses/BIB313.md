## Structure

### Chapter 1: 1 - Unchristian Conduct and Attitudes (1 Corinthians 1–4)

- **Introduction and Background to 1 Corinthians (1:1–9)**
  - `1.1.1` Summarize facts related to the authorship of 1 Corinthians.
  - `1.1.2` Identify the problematic questions and issues Paul addresses in this letter.
  - `1.1.3` Describe how the Corinthian church could be called a blessed church.
- **Divisions in the Church: Causes and Results (1:10–3:23)**
  - `1.2.1` Explain how and why exalting certain persons caused divisions at Corinth.
  - `1.2.2` Clarify how the Corinthians misunderstood the wisdom of God.
  - `1.2.3` Discuss the effect of divisions on spiritual maturity.
- **Divisions in the Church: The Cure (4:1–21)**
  - `1.3.1` Describe Paul’s view of ministry as stewardship before God.
  - `1.3.2` Summarize Paul’s instructions to the Corinthians to remedy their divisions.

### Chapter 2: 2 - Christian Conduct (1 Corinthians 5–7)

- **Church Discipline (5:1–13)**
  - `2.1.1` Describe the moral situation that called for immediate discipline.
  - `2.1.2` Identify four reasons church discipline is needed today.
- **Legal Disputes and Immorality (6:1–20)**
  - `2.2.1` Explain how and why Paul uses eschatological grounds to rebuke the Corinthians.
  - `2.2.2` Identify and illustrate ways Christians’ end-time destiny should affect their present conduct.
- **Marriage (7:1–40)**
  - `2.3.1` Explain why the Corinthians failed to understand God’s plan for sexual fulfillment in marriage.
  - `2.3.2` Summarize Paul’s advice to those who consider remaining unmarried.
  - `2.3.3` Explain why Paul counsels against divorce.

### Chapter 3: 3 - Christian Choices (1 Corinthians 8–10)

- **Christian Freedom and Meat Sacrificed to Idols (8–9)**
  - `3.1.1` Describe the problem regarding meat offered to idols.
  - `3.1.2` Explain how knowledge should govern Christian freedom.
- **The Example of the Israelites (10:1–11:1)**
  - `3.2.1` Identify factors that led to Israel’s idolatry and that exemplify what believers are not to do.
  - `3.2.2` Explain why Paul issues a strong warning against participating in pagan worship.

### Chapter 4: 4 - Christian Worship (1 Corinthians 11:2–12:11)

- **Instructions on Worship (11:2–16)**
  - `4.1.1` Describe the problem regarding worship that prompted Paul’s teaching on headship.
  - `4.1.2` Identify the three sets of relationships Paul uses to illustrate headship.
- **Instructions on the Lord’s Supper (11:17–34)**
  - `4.2.1` Identify principles that should govern how believers partake of the Lord’s Supper.
- **Instructions on Spiritual Gifts (12:1–11)**
  - `4.3.1` Describe how Christology serves as a criterion for judging prophecy.
  - `4.3.2` Summarize principles Paul taught about spiritual gifts.

### Chapter 5: 5 - Christian Relationships and Spiritual Gifts (1 Corinthians 12:12–14:40)

- **Spiritual Gifts and the Body (12:12–13:13)**
  - `5.1.1` Explain why Paul chose the metaphor of the body to teach about spiritual gifts.
  - `5.1.2` Clarify how love is the “most excellent way” in regard to spiritual gifts.
- **Gifts of Prophecy and Tongues (14:1–40)**
  - `5.2.1` Explain why spiritual gifts are given to the Church.
  - `5.2.2` Identify the sign value of tongues compared to prophecy.
  - `5.2.3` Summarize key principles to ensure that spiritual gifts edify the local church.

### Chapter 6: 6 - Christian Doctrine (1 Corinthians 15–16)

- **Truths About the Resurrection (15:1–58)**
  - `6.1.1` Describe the doctrinal error that caused Paul to teach on the resurrection.
  - `6.1.2` Identify what is at stake if Christ was not bodily raised from the dead.
  - `6.1.3` Explain the event that will signal Christ’s ultimate conquest of death and the realization of the Christian’s hope.
- **Concluding Matters and Instructions (16:1–24)**
  - `6.2.1` Explain how Paul uses commendation to prepare a warm reception for his associates.
  - `6.2.2` Identify principles related to making disciples.

### Chapter 7: 7 - Paul’s Recent Past Revisited (2 Corinthians 1:1–2:11)

- **Introduction to 2 Corinthians (1:1–2)**
  - `7.1.1` Summarize the historical circumstances that led Paul to write 2 Corinthians.
  - `7.1.2` 
- **Comfort in Affliction (1:3–11)**
  - `7.2.1` Identify truths Paul teaches that can help a Christian survive trials.
  - `7.2.2` Clarify why God allows trials in believers’ lives.
- **Paul Defends His Change in Plans (1:12–2:11)**
  - `7.3.1` Analyze Paul’s response to unfair and false accusations, and identify principles to help believers deal with such situations today.
  - `7.3.2` Identify truths or principles Paul teaches about church discipline.

### Chapter 8: 8 - Paul’s Apostolic Ministry (2 Corinthians 2:12–7:16)

- **The Greatness of Paul’s Ministry (2:12–4:6)**
  - `8.1.1` Discuss the relationship between uncertainty and faith.
  - `8.1.2` Explain how Paul drew confidence from God.
  - `8.1.3` Contrast the old covenant of law with the new covenant.
- **Ministry’s Trials and Triumph (4:7–5:9)**
  - `8.2.1` Examine the paradoxes of value in earthen vessels and of life through death.
- **Ministry’s Nature and Function (5:10–6:10)**
  - `8.3.1` Indicate how both fear and love motivated Paul in apostolic ministry.
  - `8.3.2` Explain why Paul is not ashamed at his sufferings endured for the sake of Christ.
- **Ministry’s Intimacy and Joy (6:11–7:16)**
  - `8.4.1` Describe Paul’s joy in his relationship with the Corinthians.
  - `8.4.2` Identify how Paul safeguarded and restored his relationship with the Corinthians.

### Chapter 9: 9 - The Macedonian Collection (2 Corinthians 8–9)

- **The Grace of Giving (8:1–9:5)**
  - `9.1.1` Summarize what the Macedonian giving teaches about Christian stewardship.
  - `9.1.2` Apply principles from Paul’s methods of financial management.
  - `9.1.3` Analyze 2 Corinthians 9:1–4, and explain how Paul’s commendation and praise aided motivation.
- **Principles of Giving (9:6–15)**
  - `9.2.1` Explain why 2 Corinthians 9:6 does not guarantee a giver’s prosperity.
  - `9.2.2` List principles of benevolence that Paul taught the Corinthians.

### Chapter 10: 10 - Paul Confronts His Critics (2 Corinthians 10:1–11:15)

- **True Apostolic Authority (10:1–11)**
  - `10.1.1` Assess Paul’s opponents’ criticism and misunderstanding of apostolic authority.
  - `10.1.2` Identify truths Paul teaches about apostolic authority.
- **To Boast or Not to Boast (10:12–18)**
  - `10.2.1` Analyze 2 Corinthians 10:12–18, and describe boasting that is acceptable for believers.
- **False Apostles Unmasked (11:1–15)**
  - `10.3.1` Describe Paul’s passion and goal for the Corinthians.
  - `10.3.2` Describe how Paul’s opponents would evaluate Paul.
  - `10.3.3` Summarize Paul’s assessment of his opponents’ true identity and the character of their ministry.

### Chapter 11: 11 - Strange Boastings (2 Corinthians 11:16–13:14)

- **Paul’s Boasts (11–12)**
  - `11.1.1` Describe ways in which Paul’s boasts differ from those of his opponents.
  - `11.1.2` Explain the importance of visions and revelations to Paul’s opponents and why Paul boasts of those the Lord gave him.
  - `11.1.3` Identify the things that mark Paul as a true apostle of Christ.
- **Paul’s Final Warnings (13:1–14)**
  - `11.2.1` Explain how Paul’s passion results in a warning and the threat of discipline.
